## 1. Why This System Cuts Out 90% of the Noise

Most students fail or burn out during campus placements because they follow generic, unfocused advice:
* **The Over-Preparation Trap:** Grinding 400–500 random LeetCode Hard problems (Segment Trees, Digit DP, Heavy-Light Decomposition) designed for 35+ LPA FAANG interviews, while neglecting SQL joins, OOP pillars, and paper-based coding.
* **The Under-Preparation Trap:** Memorizing textbook definitions without knowing how to write clean $O(n)$ code on paper, defend resume projects, or navigate Managerial/HR elimination traps.

### The Sub-10 LPA Pattern Reality
In the **3.5 LPA to 10 LPA bracket**, companies do not invent brand-new competitive programming puzzles. Because they interview thousands of candidates across tight 30-to-45-minute windows, **90% of their evaluation follows a predictable, repeatable pattern**:
1. **Online Assessments (OAs)** recycle the same 15–20 core algorithmic patterns (Sliding Window, Two Pointers, HashMaps, Prefix Sums, Number Theory, Basic Grids/DP) paired with Quantitative Aptitude and SQL.
2. **Face-to-Face Technical Rounds** rely on the exact same **Top 20 live-coding tasks** (written on paper or Notepad) and the **Top 12 Core CS concepts** (OOPs & DBMS account for 75% of all theory questions).
3. **Managerial & HR Rounds** test specific behavioral filters (relocation flexibility, night shifts, legacy project readiness, and team conflict handling) where a single wrong answer triggers silent rejection.

## 4. Why 2 Months (60 Days) Is Mathematically Enough

Studying this entire system from scratch takes **~180 to 190 focused hours**. At **3 to 3.5 hours per day**, you will complete every single problem, SQL query, Core CS concept, and project script in **8 weeks (60 days)**.

### The 8-Week (60-Day) Master Execution Plan

#### Weeks 1 & 2: The Non-Negotiable Core (Tier S + OOPs + SQL)
* **Daily Routine:** 2 hours DSA + 1 hour OOPs/SQL + 30 mins Quantitative Aptitude.
* **DSA Target:** Master all **18 Tier S problems** (Two Sum, Kadane's, Dutch National Flag, Sliding Window, String Frequency/Anagrams, Linked List Reversal, Binary Search, Primes/GCD).
* **Core CS Target:** Master the **Top 6 Core CS questions** (4 Pillars of OOPs with project examples, Overloading vs. Overriding, Abstract Class vs. Interface, SQL Joins, 2nd Highest Salary, Primary/Unique/Foreign Keys).
* **Outcome by Day 14:** Ready to clear 3.5–4.5 LPA technical interviews.

#### Weeks 3 & 4: The 6–7.5 LPA Differentiators (Tier A + DBMS + Paper Coding)
* **Daily Routine:** 2 hours DSA + 1 hour DBMS + 30 mins Logical Reasoning/Aptitude.
* **DSA Target:** Complete all **18 Tier A problems** (Boyer-Moore Majority Element, Merge Intervals, Prefix/Suffix Product, Spiral Matrix, 3Sum, Monotonic Stack, Binary Tree Traversals, Merge/Quick Sort, 1D DP).
* **Core CS Target:** Complete the remaining **Top 12 Core CS questions** (ACID, `DELETE` vs `TRUNCATE` vs `DROP`, Normalization 1NF–BCNF, Indexing) + all **6 Live SQL/OOP Snippets**.
* **Paper-Coding Drill:** Hand-write the **Top 20 Live Coding problems** on blank paper without an IDE.

#### Weeks 5 & 6: The 8–10 LPA Clinchers (Tier B + OS/CN + Project Defense)
* **Daily Routine:** 2 hours DSA + 1 hour OS/CN & Project Pitch + 30 mins Timed Aptitude.
* **DSA Target:** Complete all **18 Tier B problems** (Next Permutation, Set Matrix Zeroes, Trapping Rain Water, Binary Search on Answer Space, Min Stack, BST Validation, Heaps, Number of Islands, 0/1 Knapsack).
* **Core CS Target:** Secondary tables for **Operating Systems** (Process vs. Thread, Deadlock, Virtual Memory) and **Computer Networks** (TCP vs. UDP, DNS + 3-Way Handshake, HTTP Status Codes).
* **Project Defense:** Write and rehearse your 90-second architecture pitch, tech-stack trade-offs, and one real debugging story from **Phase 2 of the Interview Blueprint**.

#### Weeks 7 & 8: Full Shield & Mock Conversion (Tier C + MR/HR Mastery)
* **Daily Routine:** 1.5 hours Tier C DSA + 1 hour Full Revision + 1 hour Timed Mock OAs.
* **DSA Target:** Complete **Tier C problems** (LRU Cache, Rotting Oranges, Cycle Detection, Topological Sort, Dijkstra, LCS/LIS, Backtracking).
* **MR & HR Target:** Memorize the STAR-method conflict story, SDLC/Agile workflow, and the non-negotiable HR scripts (relocation, night shifts, 5-year vision, company research).
* **Final Polish:** Take 4 full-length timed mock Online Assessments (Aptitude + 2 Coding problems in 90 minutes).

## 5. The 5 Golden Rules to Guarantee Your Offer Letter

1. **The Missing Piece — Never Skip Daily Aptitude:** 70% to 80% of candidates are eliminated in Stage 1 (the Online Assessment). Your DSA and Core CS mastery only matters once you enter the interview room. Spend **30 minutes every single day** on Quantitative Aptitude and Logical Reasoning.
2. **Understand Logic Over Code Memorization:** Do not memorize lines of code. Understand *why* a HashMap reduces $O(n^2)$ to $O(n)$, *why* `mid = low + (high - low) / 2` prevents overflow, and *how* pointers move during a dry run.
3. **Practice on Paper and Plain Notepad:** In 85% of on-campus interviews, you will be asked to write code on a physical sheet of paper or a plain text editor without syntax highlighting or auto-complete.
4. **Steer the Interview in Minute 1:** Whatever you say in the last two sentences of *"Tell me about yourself"* dictates the next 15 minutes of the interview. Always bait the panel with your strongest project and your best core subject (OOPs or DBMS).
5. **Never Self-Eliminate in HR:** When asked about relocation, night shifts, service agreements, or legacy/support roles, give an immediate, enthusiastic **"Yes."** Never mention plans for higher studies (GATE/CAT/MS) during a placement interview.
